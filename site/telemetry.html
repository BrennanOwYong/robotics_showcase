Runtime sequence

One event. Four controlled boundaries.

The important change is not only the JSON shape. It is the ownership of each step.

01Detect The client observes a robot state change.
02Publish It sends the record on its own outbound topic.
03Refine The hub validates identity and produces the canonical event.
04React Each client receives the broadcast and can display, log, or act.
