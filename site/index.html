Engineering approach

Make the loop visible.

A robotics system is more than an algorithm. It is the full loop from sensor input to decision, command, physical response, and measured result.

01

Model the boundary.
Keep sensors, decisions, commands, and simulation interfaces explicit.

02

Keep it tunable.
Expose gains, thresholds, frames, timing, and launch choices.

03

Explain the outcome.
Record enough state to understand success, failure, retry, or fallback.
