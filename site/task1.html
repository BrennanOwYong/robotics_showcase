Problem

Keep every ROS node informed.

Clients need identity, join and leave events, shared messages, active-user counts, and recent history without direct client-to-client coupling.

Built

One broadcast, many deliveries.

The hub validates one record, publishes it once on /ros_chat/broadcast, and ROS 2 delivers a copy to every subscribed node.

Architecture decisions

Broadcast is the system boundary.

One publisher. The hub publishes one validated record on the shared broadcast topic.

Many subscribers. Every ROS 2 client subscribes and receives its own delivered copy.

Persistence is separate. The hub also appends recent history, but storage is not the delivery path.

Ownership stays clear. The hub validates the sender before it broadcasts.
